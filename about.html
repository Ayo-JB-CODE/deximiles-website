About Us

Incorporated in 2019, dexi & miles Ltd with RC: 1553342. It is an Indigenous Building and Civil Engineering Solution Company focused on providing a broad range of services to the Telecommunication, Engineering, Oil & Gas, Construction and Built environment in Nigeria and beyond. Our local staff and international partners have varied experience which cuts across all transaction types including but not limited to turn-key development and framework, formal partnering, design & build, and traditional contractual agreements, in Nigeria and open to opportunities abroad. Our business philosophy has been founded on the principle of analysing and fully understanding each client's needs, and then working closely with them to fulfil their objectives.
