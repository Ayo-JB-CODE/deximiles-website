ABOUT DEXI & MILES

UNCOMPROMISING ENGINEERING
EXCELLENCE

Incorporated in 2019, dexi & miles Ltd with RC: 1553342. It is an Indigenous Building and Civil Engineering Solution
Company focused on providing a broad range of services to the Telecommunication, Engineering, Oil & Gas,
Construction and Built environment in Nigeria and beyond. Our local staff and international partners have varied
experience which cuts across all transaction types including but not limited to turn-key development and
framework, formal partnering, design & build, and traditional contractual agreements, in Nigeria and open to
opportunities abroad. Our business philosophy has been founded on the principle of analysing and fully
understanding each client's needs, and then working closely with them to fulfil their objectives.

At Dexi & Miles Nigeria Limited, we transform ideas into enduring structures through a comprehensive range of
construction and engineering services. From the construction of buildings, factories, warehouses, and industrial facilities

FREE STALL COW MILK BARN

This is a 50m by 30m cow milk barn construction with steel stanchions.It was designed to house about 250(two hundred and fifty cows).This project was built by Dexi and Miles limited at Ikun Dairy Farm Amure Ekiti State for Promasidor Nigeria Group.

SECURITY TOWER

This was built and delivered to Promasidor Nigeria Group by Dexi and Miles limited. The security Tower is about 15m high from the base supporting columns and 2.8m width. All steel fabrication done on project location Ikun Dairy Farm Amure Ekiti State.

STORAGE FACILITY

This was constructed by Dexi and Miles limited and delivered to Promasidor Nigeria Group. This project comprises four forty feet container caravan built and equiped with cooling facilities for storage purposes only. Project location plot 3A and B cowbell Way Lagos State.

EFFLUENT TREATMENT PLANT
DECOMMISSIONING WORKS

Effluent Treatment Plant previously built for efficient surface water treatment was decommissioned due to reduced efficiency. A new effluent Treatment Plant was commissioned immediately to treat all waste water, producing clean water in the process that will be re used in the factory.Project location plot 3A and B cowbell way Lagos State.

SECURITY HOUSE

This was a new modern standard security house with latest security gadgets installed, built and delivered to Promasidor Nigeria Group.Project location Ikun Dairy Farm Amure Ekiti State.

RAW MATERIAL WAREHOUSE
FLOOR RECONSTRUCTION

This project was undertaken and constructed by Dexi and Miles limited. The project is 900sqm in total, consisting of 6m by 6m hardstand, demolished and reconstructed with with 1:2:3 concrete mix ratio with snippet finishing.Project location cowbell way plot 3A and B ilasamaja Lagos State.

WALL LEAKAGE CORRECTION WORKS

Wall leakage corrections works was investigated and corrected by Dexi and Miles limited. This was by applying water leakage chemical sealants to close up all identified spots. Project site Raw Materials Warehouse location plot 3A and B cowbell way Lagos State.

SOS FACILITY RENOVATION

This comprises renovation of the existing facility to Promasidor Nigeria Group standard, equiped with cooling systems,HSE firefighting equipment,HSE smoke detectors equipment,HSE alarm system.A new modern standard security house, landscaping jobs, painting and general furnishings.Project location 153 MUSHIN ROAD BY LASPOTECH POLYTECHNIC.

STORAGE FACILITY

This project was undertaken and delivered to Promasidor Nigeria Group by Dexi and Miles limited. It involves removal of existing tile works and re-tiling same. It also involves correction of several underground leakages. Project location Raw Materials Warehouse plot 3A and B cowbell way Lagos State.
